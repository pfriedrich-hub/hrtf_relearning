## Soldering of miniature in-ear microphones for HRIR recording
## Date: 07/09/2026 (started), continued in the following days;
##       ~1 soldering session per month planned

### Project:
- Assembly of miniature in-ear microphones for individual HRIR (head-related
  impulse response) recordings in the Department of Neuropsychology
  (auditory spatial perception / HRTF relearning, Schoenwiesner lab).
  The microphones are worn in the blocked ear canal and record the sound of
  loudspeaker sweeps at the listener's own ears; the resulting individual
  HRTFs are used for virtual acoustic stimulus presentation over headphones.
  Commercial in-ear probe microphones of the required size are not available
  off the shelf, so the capsules are wired and encapsulated in-house.

### Workflow:
- Preparation: Miniature electret capsules ([Modell/Hersteller einfuegen],
  diameter [x] mm) and thin shielded 2-wire cable were cut to length, the cable
  ends stripped and pre-tinned. Capsules fixed in a helping-hands/third-hand
  holder.
- Soldering: Signal and ground of the shielded cable soldered to the capsule
  terminals at [xxx] degrees C with a fine tip and thin lead-free solder
  ([Lot einfuegen]), contact time kept below ~2 s per joint to avoid heat
  damage to the electret element. Work carried out at the soldering station
  with active fume extraction.
- Strain relief: Joints insulated and mechanically relieved with heat-shrink
  tubing (hot air station).
- Connectorisation: Cable ends soldered to [Stecker einfuegen, z.B. 3.5 mm
  Klinke / XLR / LEMO] for connection to the microphone preamplifier of the
  TDT recording system.
- Testing: Continuity and short-circuit check with the multimeter, then
  functional test on the recording rig (bias voltage, noise floor, and a test
  sweep recorded with the freefield/TDT setup).
- Finishing: Capsules embedded in [Silikon-Ohrstoepsel / Otoplastik einfuegen]
  for the blocked-ear-canal fit.
- Number of units: [n] microphone pairs assembled / repaired.

### Equipment used in the MakerLab:
- Soldering station with fine tip + fume extraction, hot air station,
  helping hands, wire stripper, multimeter
